About Us

Democratizing precision agriculture in Peru

SumaqAgro was created to bridge the technology gap in potato and coffee-growing regions without requiring expensive ground sensors. We convert open satellite imagery and agronomic data into accessible monitoring, real-cost accounting, and quality certification tools, allowing farmers and agricultural organizations to make technically supported decisions.

VALUE-GENERATING TECHNOLOGY

Solutions & Features

Simple and powerful tools for better decision-making in the field.

Satellite Vigor & Moisture Monitoring

Dashboard based on open satellite imagery to calculate vegetation indices (NDVI) and water stress without buying or installing physical ground hardware.

Reduces crop losses caused by undetected frosts and droughts by up to 25%.

Cost Accounting per Lot

Module to log day labor, agricultural inputs, and machinery in real time. Automatically calculates unit cost per quintal or ton and sets your financial break-even point.

Avoid selling at a loss to middlemen and consolidate field logbooks 50% faster.

Digital Harvest Quality Certification

Generates verifiable digital reports with potato size grading or coffee cup scores, protecting your yield from arbitrary price cuts at collection points.

Formal proof to negotiate price premiums in specialty and export markets.

Agronomic Prescriptions & Alerts

Centralized system for preventive technical advice and digital phytosanitary recipes to fight pests like coffee rust or Andean potato weevil.

Allows technical advisors to supervise 40% more hectares without constant travel.
