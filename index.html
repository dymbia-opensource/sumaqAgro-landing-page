Frequently asked questions

Before you start

Can't find your answer? Write to us and we will reply within one business day.

Do I need to buy sensors or drones?

No. SumaqAgro works only with open satellite imagery (Sentinel-2) and the data you log. You only need a phone or a computer with internet.

Does it work with poor signal in the field?

Yes. With offline mode you can log expenses and reports without connection; they sync automatically when you get signal again.

Which crops are supported?

We currently focus on Andean potato and specialty coffee, including potato size grading and coffee cup profiles for certificates.

How often is the satellite map updated?

Sentinel-2 revisits each area about every 5 days. On very cloudy days the image may take longer, and we always show the capture date.

What does the Seed Plan include?

The Seed Plan costs S/49 per month or S/490 per year and includes up to 4 registered plots, crop seasons per plot, NDVI and NDWI indices, expense, day-labor and freight records, cost calculations and agroclimatic alerts.

Who can see my plot and cost data?

Only you and the people you invite, such as your cooperative or advisor. We do not sell personal data. Read our Privacy Policy.
