Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) are small pieces of data saved on your device when you visit a website, to make it work and remember your preferences.

2. What this website uses

  • Preferences: We save your language choice (ES/EN) in your browser's local storage so you do not have to select it again.
  • Third-party content: The demo video is loaded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode only after you press play.
  • Web application: Once you sign in, the SumaqAgro app uses essential cookies to keep your session active and secure.

3. How to manage cookies

You can delete or block cookies and local storage at any time from your web browser settings.
