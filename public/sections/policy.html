Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

At SumaqAgro we collect personal data and agricultural information to provide our precision agriculture services, including:

  • Identification data: Name, email address, and contact number.
  • Location data: Georeferencing of registered plots or farms.
  • Agronomic information: Crop records and operational costs entered by the user.

2. Use of Information

The collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Process satellite images and vegetation indices (NDVI and NDWI).
  • Generate digital production reports and certificates.
  • Enable technical communication and agronomic support.

3. Data Protection and Storage

We implement technical security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. We do not share or sell personal data to third parties. This landing page does not collect personal data.

4. Contact

For inquiries regarding the processing of your data, you can email us at contacto@sumaqagro.pe.
